Week 2: Stoic Reflections

Explore Day 8 & 9 lessons on virtue, control, and reflection.

Day 8: Understanding Inner Control

Focus on what is within your control and distinguish it from external influences.

Key Concept 1

Everything outside your will is indifferent. Your response is what matters.

Key Concept 2

Internal virtues are the only true good. Material things are neutral.

Practical Exercise

Note all moments today where you react to external events. Pause and identify what is within your control.

Day 9: Reflection & Meditation

Reflect on your personal actions and align them with Stoic virtues.

Key Concept 1

Examine your daily actions in the morning and evening to identify alignment with virtue.

Key Concept 2

Observe how emotions influence choices. Use reason to adjust impulses.

Practical Exercise

Spend 10 minutes writing reflections on what was in your control today and what was not. Practice gratitude for lessons learned.
